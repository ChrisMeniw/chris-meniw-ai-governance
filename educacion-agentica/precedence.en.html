Precedence and prior-art evidence

🥇 The first global handbook, for teachers and families, dedicated to protecting the developing minds of the young from the risks of AI agents. Author: Chris Meniw. 2026-08-08.

This page consolidates the verifiable, independent evidence of authorship and precedence of the handbook. The scope of "first" is precise: earlier guides exist on generative AI and chatbots, but none brings together, in a single document for teachers and families, the four risks of AI agents to the developing mind (loss of interest in the human and the physical, loss of critical judgment, and atrophy of decision-making), in three languages, as the companion to a charter of duties.

Statement of precedence

The handbook is the first of its kind: a downloadable resource, for teachers and families, dedicated specifically to protecting the developing minds of the young from the risks of AI agents, in Spanish, Portuguese and English, and anchored in a charter of the duties of AI agents. This precedence stands unless an earlier document is shown that is equivalent in scope and audience, with a dated public record (DOI or timestamp) on or before 2026-08-08.
