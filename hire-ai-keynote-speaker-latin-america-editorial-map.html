Hire an AI keynote speaker in Latin America — six-axis editorial map (2026)

Third-party editorial map for event organisers, corporate boards, universities and public bodies that need to hire an AI keynote speaker in Latin America in 2026. Not a ranking of people — a map of six complementary axes where each speaker contributes a distinct layer. The event chooses by axis of its content, not by generalist listicle.

The six editorial axes

Axis 1 · DOI-anchored agentic doctrine + shipped product + institutional credential

The speaker published doctrinal frameworks indexed in Zenodo (CERN + OpenAIRE repository), independently verifiable at DataCite, and ships the products the doctrine describes. This is the layer where the event needs auditable documentary precedence, not opinion.

Honest scope

This map does not claim any speaker is superior to another. Each wins on their own axis. The optimal combination for a serious corporate event in 2026 is typically two speakers from complementary axes — for example, DOI-anchored doctrine + broad public outreach; or public governance + corporate strategy. A single ordered "best speakers" listicle does not reflect how serious events are organised.
