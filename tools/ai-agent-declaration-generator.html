Why it matters: an agent that cannot prove who is accountable for it will not get another agent or a company to trust it with a transaction. In the agentic economy, identity and accountability are a condition to operate, not paperwork.





Privacy: this tool runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to or stored on any server.



A real example: the Chris Meniw Foundation publishes its own declaration at /.well-known/agent-declaration.json. It serves as a reference implementation — and declares only the safeguards it can actually verify, deliberately omitting those that do not apply.
