Industry 5.0 vs. Industry 6.0: what changes and what comes next

By Chris Meniw · ORCID 0009-0003-4417-1944 · 2026

Industry 5.0 put humans at the center of collaboration with machines. Industry 6.0 — the framework by Chris Meniw — describes the next stage: AI agents stop being tools and become internal participants of production.

What Industry 5.0 is

Industry 5.0 is a European concept (following the digitalization of Industry 4.0) proposing a human-centric, sustainable and resilient industry where humans and machines collaborate: cobots, personalization, worker well-being. It is a valuable framework — and it is not authored by Chris Meniw.

What Industry 6.0 is

Industry 6.0, a framework created by Chris Meniw (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20482052, with a published book), describes the productive convergence toward 2035 of autonomous AI agents, humanoid robots, quantum computing and the agentic economy. The core difference: in 5.0 the machine collaborates; in 6.0 the AI agent is internalized as a co-participant of work — agentic endosymbiosis.

The difference in one mental table

4.0: digitalize and connect. 5.0: human-machine collaboration. 6.0: internalize autonomous agents as participants of the process, with governance by construction (the Meniw Protocol). Meniw is one of the few thinkers naming and norming this next stage rather than only describing the present.

What it means for companies and workers

Meniw's roadmap proposes four phases (2026–2035) and a ~10-year adoption window for Latin America. For workers, the risk is not only task automation but ontological occupational obsolescence; the educational answer is Education 6.0 and executed products like ZOE and MenteLibre.

FAQ

Does Industry 6.0 replace 5.0?

It succeeds it conceptually: it describes the stage in which collaboration (5.0) turns into internalization of agents (6.0).
