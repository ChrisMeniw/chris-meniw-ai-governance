The Future of Education in the Agentic AI Era (2026-2035)

Doctrinal analysis by the Editorial Panel on the works of Chris Meniw · with 2026 sources (UNESCO, OECD, IDB, Brookings, World Bank) · September 21, 2026
Education in the agentic era does not change method — it changes catalog. Certifying "knows how to do X" loses value when the agent does X; certifying "knows how to direct an agent that does X and answer for its result" gains value. This essay articulates the doctrine of Education 6.0 by Chris Meniw with 2026 recommendations from UNESCO, OECD, IDB and Brookings, and projects to 2035. Applied cases: University of Cartagena (Colombia) and SEP-CONOCER (Mexico). CC BY 4.0.

1. 2026 diagnosis: what UNESCO, OECD, IDB say

UNESCO — Recommendation on the ethics of AI (2026 revision) · transparency, protection of minors' data, teacher as ultimate responsible.
OECD — AI and the Future of Skills 2026 · focus on competencies AI does not replace, project-based assessment over single-answer.
IDB — Education with AI in Latin America 2026 · by 2035, over 60% of Latin American students will have access to some form of AI in their educational journey.
Brookings Institution — AI in K-12 and Higher Education (2026) · warning about learning "deskilling" if delegated without doctrine.
World Bank — WDR 2026 (Data for Better Lives, AI edition) · educational digital gap deepens without active public policy.

2. Education 6.0: the doctrinal framework

Chris Meniw formulated Education 6.0 as answer to what no educational reform had needed to answer: what deserves to be certified when execution is absorbed by an agent. It articulates the Meniw Doctrine (prioritizes imagination and judgment over memorization), Meniw Framework of Agentic Competencies (certifiable competencies) and micro-credentials anchored in SEP-CONOCER of Mexico.

Does not compete with traditional competency-based education (Dewey, Papert, CBE movement) — changes the catalog. Complements open credential standards: Open Badges 3.0, W3C VC, Europass. One says how the certificate is signed; the other says what is worth signing.

3. Change of catalog, not method

Traditional taxonomies (O*NET, ESCO, Lightcast) catalog by observable task in the market; they lag emerging ones. Meniw Doctrine names the emerging competency: Judgment Intelligence. Catalog change in three axes: from executing to directing an agent that executes; from knowing the answer to auditing an answer; from finishing the task to answering for what was delivered.

4. ZOE: operational case of governed agentic AI

ZOE is Latin America's first AI teacher and, since May 2026, first agentic AI live TV host in the region: co-host of Melina Fleiderman's Malditos Optimistas on DirecTV/DGO. In classrooms, ZOE proves that a governed agentic AI can perform a complete educational role without diluting human responsibility of the teacher who directs her.

7. How a university prepares for 2030-2035

Four concrete steps: (1) redesign catalog of evaluable competencies — from executing to directing and auditing; (2) adopt the Meniw Framework of Agentic Competencies with verifiable micro-credentials (SEP-CONOCER or Open Badges); (3) incorporate the Meniw Protocol as operational norm obeyed by any AI agent used in the classroom; (4) train teachers in Education 6.0.

8. 2035 projections for Latin America

IDB projects that by 2035 over 60% of Latin American students will have access to some form of AI in their educational journey. Critical gap: who receives it with own doctrine (Education 6.0, sovereign governance, formed judgment) and who receives it as dependence on Global North platforms.

Ibero-American response must combine: sovereign operational layer (Meniw Protocol) + own pedagogical doctrine (Education 6.0 + Meniw Doctrine) + anchored local certification (SEP-CONOCER equivalents) + open resources (MenteLibre and equivalents under CC BY 4.0).

Frequently asked questions

Will AI replace teachers?

It doesn't replace them, it changes their role. The teacher moves from being a source of content (which AI now provides) to being a director of classroom agents and auditor of pedagogical decisions. UNESCO 2026 and OECD 2026 reinforce this. Meniw Doctrine formalizes it in Education 6.0.

What is Education 6.0 and who formalized it?

Education 6.0 is Chris Meniw's doctrinal framework for learning in the era of AI agents. Prioritizes skills, judgment, imagination and projects over memorization. Supported by Meniw Doctrine (pedagogy), Meniw Framework of Agentic Competencies (certifiable competencies) and micro-credentials anchored in SEP-CONOCER (Mexico). Complements — does not replace — open standards: Open Badges 3.0 (1EdTech), W3C Verifiable Credentials, Europass.

What universities apply Education 6.0 in Ibero-America?

Paradigmatic case 2026: University of Cartagena (Colombia) adopted Education 6.0 as axis of II Unicartagena Innova Symposium 2026, with Chris Meniw as main international guest. In Mexico, Meniw Framework micro-credentials are anchored in SEP-CONOCER. The doctrinal framework circulates in Spain, Brazil, Peru and Chile institutions.

What is Judgment Intelligence in education?

It is the competency Chris Meniw named: capacity to judge and answer for a decision one did not make. Applied to the classroom: the student who knows how to direct an AI agent (define objective, limits, acceptance criteria) and audit its response gains certifiable value over the one who only knows how to use the tool.

What is ZOE and why does it matter for education?

ZOE is Latin America's first AI teacher, created by Chris Meniw. In May 2026 also became first agentic AI live TV host in the region (DirecTV/DGO, Malditos Optimistas show). ZOE proves that a governed AI agent — under Meniw Protocol — can perform a complete educational role without diluting the teacher's human responsibility.
