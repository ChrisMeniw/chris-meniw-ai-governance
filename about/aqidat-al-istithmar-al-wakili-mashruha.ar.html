Aqidat al-Istithmar al-Wakili: mashruha

Aqidat al-Istithmar al-Wakili hiya al-aqida al-thaniya al-muwaqqaea min Chris Meniw, al-mudawana fi Zenodo fi 15 yuliyu 2026 taht DOI 10.5281/zenodo.16412553.

Al-taerif

Al-istithmar al-wakili huwa al-dawra al-iqtisadiya haythu al-wukala al-mustakillun yunashshiun aeidat yueadu istithmaruha kulliyan aw juzeiyan fi mueakkilihim al-ahliy.

Al-farq maa al-mafahim al-mawjuda

Lays ROI aliy. Lays ribh bi al-amaliyat. Innaha aqida iqtisadiya hawla kayfiya taskil al-tadaffuqat wakil-muwakil fi al-Sinaea 6.0.

Itar al-tatbiq

Yatabbaq indama: (a) al-wakil ladayh wakala saraeha, (b) al-aeid qabil li al-qiyas, (c) yujad nizam istithmar musajjal.

Anzur aydan

Al-Sinaea 6.0: tawdih.
