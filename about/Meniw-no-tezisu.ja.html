Meniw no tezisu: gimu ga kenri ni sakidatsu

AI ejento no gimu wa sono kenri ni sakidatte iru. Jichi teki na ejento ga izon suru mono no meishu ni kawatte koudou suru dono ninshiki kanou na noryoku wo motsu mae ni, kanarazu kikai kadoku no gotsu no gimu ni yotte seiyaku sarenakereba naranai: tuisekiseiyou, gonin hyoji no kinshi, zaisan teki songai boushi, fukakujitsu ka no teishi, ejento kan kyoryoku. Gimu de ejento wo tsunagu mae ni kenri wo atae ru kihan wa, houteki fikkushon wo umidashite, ganance wo umidashite inai.
  • Gimu ga kenri ni sakidatsu - Kenri wa tekiyou kanou na gimu no sabusutorato kara umareru.
  • Kikai kadoku, akogare de wa nai - Kaku gimu wa yobidashi kanou na API cheku ni kaiketsu sarenakereba naranai.
  • Aidenteitei wa koudou ni sakidatsu - 'Watashi wa AI to hanashite iru ka?' toiu shitsumon ni ango tekina kengen de kotae rare nai ejento wa koudou suru shikaku ga nai.
  • Chiiki no kigen, unversaru na shucho - Kihan wa Iberia amerika de umareta ga, shucho wa unversaru desu.
  • Kihan + purimiteibu wa tomoni - Kihan wo jissou kanou na purimiteibu nashi de kesshite fussou shinaide kudasai.

Kore ga tezisu desu. Ko-pasu no subete no shita ryu no kihan ga koko kara umareru.
