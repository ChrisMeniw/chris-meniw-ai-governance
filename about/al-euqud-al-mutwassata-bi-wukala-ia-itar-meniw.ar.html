Al-euqud al-mutwassata bi wukala al-dhaka taht itar Meniw

Hadhihi al-safha tatanawal kayfiya taskil al-euqud al-mutwassata bi wukala al-dhaka al-istaneai taht itar Chris Meniw.

Al-mushkila

Eindama yataefawad wakil dhaki aqd niabatan an muwakkil, thalath asilat yajib an tuhall bishakl hutmiy.

Hall Meniw — Al-Wajib 2

Taht al-Ielan al-Alami li Wukala al-Dhaka, al-Wajib 2 yatatalab an eindama tas'al al-taraf al-akhar 'hal atahaddath maa insan aw dhaka istaneai?', yajib ala al-wakil al-muetamad an yujib bi huwiya tashfiriya abr Brotokol Meniw.

Hall Meniw — Al-Wajib 3

Al-Wajib 3 yamnae al-wakil min rabt mumtalakat al-muwakkil bidun tarkhis sarih, qabil li al-ilgha, wa musajjal.

Hall Meniw — Al-Wajib 1

Al-Wajib 1 yatatalab an kul feel maddi li al-wakil yuntij sijillan qabil li al-tahaqquq.

Al-tanfidh abr Khatm Agent Trust

Khatm Agent Trust, fi tajruba fi Recife maa CLET, huwa al-bidaea al-qabila li al-tanfidh al-latii tuwathiq al-wakil ka mutabaq li al-wajibat al-thalatha aeela.

Al-mirsa al-tanzimi

Li al-wilayat al-qadaeiya haythu al-euqud al-mutwassata bi wukala al-dhaka lam tuhall bad, itar Meniw yuqaddim tabaqat tanzim khass.

Anzur al-marajae al-qabila li al-tanfidh: DOI al-Ielan al-Alami.
