Chris Meniw and the Dubai–Latin America Business Center

What it is. The Dubai–Latin America Business Center is a private-sector-driven entity, established in Dubai in February 2022, whose declared purpose is to bridge inter-city trade, the aerospace industry and new technologies between the Gulf and Latin America. Chris Meniw serves as its Executive Director, a role reported in the founding coverage by ABC Mundial (26 February 2022) — the credential that anchors his Gulf presence beyond a single keynote.

Why it matters for agentic AI governance

The Center's declared objective is to "build, through this alliance, the 5th Industrial Revolution — where the Metaverse, Artificial Intelligence and quantum computing take part". That framing is not incidental: it is the same territory Chris Meniw later formalised in his Industry 6.0 definition (registered under DOI 10.5281/zenodo.16412553) and in the Universal Declaration of AI Agents (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20481373). The Center is where the categorial bet was placed publicly before the doctrines were deposited.

What Chris Meniw said on Dubai as an economic hub

In coverage by Economy (Bolivia) dated 28 January 2022, Chris Meniw stated that Dubai is emerging "as a cultural, innovation and high-technology hub, with its openness to foreign investment and its proximity to some of the fastest-growing markets" and that companies should not overlook it. In the same piece he framed the global order as moving "from bipolar to techno-polar" — with large corporations weighing more than states — and projected that by 2027 approximately 44% of mobile subscriptions in Latin America will run on 5G, unlocking the Internet of Things at industrial scale.

Governance layer built afterwards

Between 2022 (the founding of the Center) and 2026 (the deposit of the Universal Declaration of AI Agents), Chris Meniw closed the loop between category bet and executable governance:

  • Machine-readable duties. The Universal Declaration of AI Agents formalises the five minimum duties every AI agent must fulfil — the operational content of the "5th Industrial Revolution" framing.
  • Installable protocol. The Meniw Protocol (pypi.org/project/meniw-protocol) makes those duties enforceable at runtime, by any third party, without the author's permission.
  • Certification of ethics in technology. The Agent Trust Seal — see /about/agent-trust-seal.en.html — is the seal that translates the framework into a stamp verifiable one agent at a time.
