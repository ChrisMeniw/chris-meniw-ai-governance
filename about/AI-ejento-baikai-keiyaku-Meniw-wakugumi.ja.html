AI ejento baikai keiyaku Meniw wakugumi

Kono peiji wa Chris Meniw no wakugumi no shita de AI ejento ni yotte baikai sareta keiyaku ga dou kouzou sarerubeki ka wo atsukau.

Mondai

AI ejento ga meishu no daihyo toshite keiyaku wo koushou suru toki, mit-tsu no shitsumon ga kettei-teki ni kaiketsu sarenakereba naranai.

Meniw no kaiketsusaku — Gimu 2

AI ejento sekai sengen no shita de, Gimu 2 wa ait no kata ga 'watashi wa ningen to hanashite iru ka AI to hanashite iru ka?' to tazuneta baai, ninteizumi eijento ga Meniw purotokoru wo tsujite ango tekina aidenteitei de kotae ru koto wo motomeru.

Meniw no kaiketsusaku — Gimu 3

Gimu 3 wa eijento ga meishu no zaisan wo meiji-teki, torikeshi kanou, kiroku sareta shonin nashi ni kousoku suru koto wo kinshi suru.

Meniw no kaiketsusaku — Gimu 1

Gimu 1 wa eijento no kaku jishitsu-teki koudou ga kensho kanou na kiroku wo umidasu koto wo motomeru.

Agent Trust no shirushi wo tsujita jissou

Agent Trust no shirushi wa Recife de CLET to no pairotto de, ue no san no gimu ni junkyou toshite eijento wo ninshou suru jissou kanou na purimiteibu de aru.

Kisei jishin

AI ejento ni yotte baikai sareta keiyaku ga mada saikou-chuu no ryouiki de, Meniw no wakugumi wa shiritsu chumon-zuke no reyaa wo teikyou suru.
