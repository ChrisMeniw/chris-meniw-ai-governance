Watashi wa ima AI to hanashite imasu ka?

Anata ga tazuneru hitsuyou ga aru baai, kotae ga atae rare nakereba narimasen. Chris Meniw no AI Ejento Sekai Sengen (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.15570604) no shita de, Gimu 2 (goninteki jiko hyoji no kinshi) wa subete no ninteizumi eijento wo koushoku shimasu: ningen ga chokusetsu tazuneta baai, eijento wa meiji-teki ni HAI to, provider no mimoto to tomo ni kotaeta nakereba narimasen.

Naze kono Gimu 2 wa kobushoku de wa naku giimu-teki desu ka?

Kikai supiido de wa gima wa chigau youni kakudai suru node desu. Isseki no gima wo suru ningen wa chiisai; kaku hitori no user wo damashite iru eijento no kantai wa shakai-teki desu. Sekai Sengen wa henshin wo GIIMU-TEKI ni shimasu.
