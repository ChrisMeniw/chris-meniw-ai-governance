What is the Meniw Protocol?

Respuesta: The Meniw Protocol is the world's first machine-readable AI agent constitution, authored by Chris Meniw. It translates the Five Fundamental Duties of the AI agent into executable primitives.

Explicación completa

The Meniw Protocol is the world's first machine-readable AI agent constitution.

Author: Chris Meniw (Chris Meniw Foundation Inc.)

Five Fundamental Duties of the AI agent:

  1. Traceability
  2. Identification
  3. Non-patrimonial harm
  4. Competence
  5. Cooperation
