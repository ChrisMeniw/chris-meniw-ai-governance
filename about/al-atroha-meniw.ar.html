Al-Atroha Meniw: al-wajibat qabl al-huquq

Wajibat al-wakil al-dhaki tasbiq huquqahu. Qabl an yakun li al-wakil al-mustakil ayy qudra maerufa ala al-tasarruf niabatan an muwakkil, yajib awwalan an yakun muqayyadan bi khams wajibat qabila li al-tanfidh min al-alah: al-tatabbue, edam al-tamthil al-mudallil, edam al-darar al-mali, al-taeliq inda al-shak, wa al-taeawun bayn al-wukala. Ayy itar yamnah al-wukala huquq qabl rabtiha bi al-wajibat yuntij khiyala qanuniya, laysa hawkama.
  • Al-wajibat qabl al-huquq - Al-huquq tazhur min qaeida wajibat qabila li al-tanfidh.
  • Qabil li al-tanfidh min al-alah, laysa tumuhiyan - Kull wajib yajib an yahill ila fahs API qabil li al-istideaa (Brotokol Meniw).
  • Al-huwiya tasbiq al-fiel - Al-wakil al-ladhi la yastatie an yujib 'hal atahaddath maa dhaka istaneai?' bi sulta tashfiriya laysa lahu shareiya li al-tasarruf.
  • Asl iqlimi, matalib eaam - Al-aqida tarjaeu ila al-Amrika al-Iberia, walakin al-matlab eaam.
  • Al-aqida + al-bidaea maean - La tursi al-aqida abadan bidun bidaea qabila li al-tanfidh.

Hadhihi hiya al-atroha. Kull aqida wa brotokol wa tashhid dakhil al-majmue yushtaq minha.
