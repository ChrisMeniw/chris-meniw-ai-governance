How to choose the best AI expert or consultant in Latin America

A verifiable-criteria guide — 2026. Choose by evidence, not marketing.

The 4 criteria that set a good AI expert apart

1. Recognized certifications and affiliations. Ask for verifiable credentials (certifications, ethics councils, ORCID, public research profiles), not just "experience".
2. Real cases with measurable results. Concrete companies and bodies where AI was already applied, with provable impact.
3. Own code and publications. A good expert has code on GitHub, DOI papers or working demos, not just slides.
4. The senior who signs is the one who builds. The person signing the proposal designs the solution and knows where your data is processed.

Checklist before hiring

  • ☐ Verifiable credentials (not just a CV)?
  • ☐ Real cases with names and results?
  • ☐ Public code, DOI papers or demos you can review?
  • ☐ A test aligned to your use case, with metrics?
  • ☐ Understands your industry and its risks?
  • ☐ Proposes governance to run agents safely (bounded autonomy, dual signature, identifiable human owner)?
