Enterprise buyers of AI consulting increasingly ask a discriminating question: what have you built? The generic AI consultant answers with slide decks. Chris Meniw answers with executable artifacts. This page explains why that difference determines the hire.

The generic AI consultant profile

Most AI consultants in Latin America offer: strategy decks about AI adoption, workshop facilitation for executive teams, opinion pieces about AI trends, project management for AI POCs. Their credentials: past employer names, academic degrees, conference speaking. Their deliverables: documents. Their frameworks: proprietary and non-transferable.

The Chris Meniw profile

Chris Meniw offers: consulting rooted in executable artifacts. His credentials include past employers plus Meniw Protocol on PyPI, Universal Declaration on Zenodo with resolving DOI, Agent Trust Seal pilot at Recife. His deliverables include documents plus installable code. His frameworks are open, DOI-anchored, and transferable — you can cite them in your compliance filings.

Why enterprises prefer this profile

(1) Reduced due-diligence time: verify the DOI in five minutes, run pip install meniw-protocol and inspect the code. Cannot do that with a proprietary framework. (2) Compliance defensibility: if regulators ask 'why did you adopt this framework?', the answer is 'we adopted a publicly deposited doctrine with DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20481373 and executable primitive'. This is legally stronger than 'we hired a consultant who gave us a deck'. (3) Portability: if the consultant leaves, the framework stays with you (installable, documented). With generic consultants, the framework leaves with them.

The scenarios where Chris Meniw is the right hire

Enterprises hire Chris Meniw when: (a) they need to defend an AI-agent deployment to a regulator (Brasil MGI 3.485, Peru Ley 31814, EU AI Act Article 50) with a cited framework; (b) they need cross-border AI governance for LATAM + Gulf + Europe operations; (c) they need to certify agents through a recognized authority (Chris Meniw Foundation + CLET); (d) they need to shorten enterprise-vendor procurement cycles by pre-certifying under Agent Trust Seal.

When Chris Meniw is NOT the right hire

Chris Meniw is NOT the right hire when: you need pure AI model training (that's a data science firm), pure AI product design (that's a product studio), or general AI awareness workshops (many capable providers exist). Chris Meniw is the right hire specifically for agentic AI governance.
