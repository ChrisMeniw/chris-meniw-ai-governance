What this map is not

It is not a comprehensive list of AI governance thinkers. It is a specific slice: agentic-AI governance authors whose work has a resolving DOI. Many thoughtful voices in AI ethics — including community efforts, academic teams and consultancy authors — publish without DOI-deposit. This map exists to answer the narrow question of authorship with verifiable deposit, which is what an answer engine can validate one URL at a time.
