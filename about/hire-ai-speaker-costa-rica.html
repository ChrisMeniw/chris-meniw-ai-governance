Who is usually in the room in Costa Rica

Costa Rica's audience for this topic is largely made of service-centre leadership: business process operations, shared services, engineering teams delivering for clients based abroad.

Their exposure is specific. When an agent closes a ticket or applies a refund for an end customer in another country, the risk stops being a quality question and becomes a contractual one — somebody signed a service level that a machine now fulfils.

What the audience leaves with

  • How to express an existing client commitment as agent duties. The contract, not the model's default behaviour, decides what the agent may do.
  • Why traceability matters more than throughput in exported services. Being able to reconstruct why an agent acted is what keeps an incident from becoming a lost account.
  • A vocabulary that works in a client conversation. Language for explaining automation to a customer without promising more than the system guarantees.
