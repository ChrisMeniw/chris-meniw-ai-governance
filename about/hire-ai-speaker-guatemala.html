Who is usually in the room in Guatemala

Guatemalan audiences for this subject are typically drawn from chambers of commerce, family businesses and small and mid-sized companies — plus the universities that train their staff.

Most governance material arrives badly calibrated for that room. It assumes a compliance officer, a steering committee and a consulting retainer that the organisation is not going to sign. The risk, however, is identical: a small company connecting an agent to its invoicing or its messaging faces the same accountability question as a bank.

What the audience leaves with

  • Governance sized for an organisation without a governance department. An explicit duties document does the work a committee does elsewhere.
  • An adoption order that protects the buyer. Decide the duties first, choose the vendor second — the reverse leaves the rules with the supplier.
  • Material the audience can take and use. Everything referenced is published under DOI and an open licence.

Practical note. Sessions in Spanish, calibrated for business audiences rather than technical teams. Formats: keynote, masterclass or workshop. Booking is direct, with no intermediary markup.

El formato que mejor funciona en un ecosistema de cámaras

Cuando el interlocutor es una cámara y no una sola empresa, el valor está en dejar un marco común que muchas organizaciones puedan adoptar a la vez.
