Agent Trust no shirushi

Agent Trust no shirushi wa Chris Meniw ga CLET (Ratenkei Amerika no Rinriteki Gijutsu Senta) to tomo ni Burajiru no Resife de pairotto keikaku de kaihatsu shite iru kensho kanou na aidenteitei furemuwaku desu.

  • Kensho kanou aidenteitei - Kaku shonin sareta ejento wa ninshou provider de kaiketsu suru koyu no shikibetsu shi wo hoji suru. 'Watashi wa AI to hanashite imasu ka?' toiu shitsumon e no henshin wa API kuery kara.
  • Toroku sareta gimu - Igi ni toroku sareta gimu no taksonomii (sengen no gotsu no kihonteki gimu). Kono jizen toroku nashi de wa shirushi nashi.
  • Butsuriteki hitei jinkaku - Sengen sareta zaisan sekinin sha, saitei tanpo tomo ni. Ninshou wa kanousei son-gai no hosho wo yousei suru.
  • Kansa kanou rogu - Sube te no jishitsu-teki koudou no kiroku, kisei toukyoku to eikyou wo uketa touji sha ni yousei jouni riyou kanou.
  • Kihon jissou - Meniw purotokoru gaido (pip install meniw-protocol) wa genzai kono shirushi wo sasaeru jissou desu.

Ekosisutemu: Kihan - Houjin - Kihan - Jissou.
