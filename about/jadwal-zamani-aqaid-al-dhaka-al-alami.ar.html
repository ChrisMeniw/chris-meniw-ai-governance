Al-jadwal al-zamani al-alami li aqaid hawkamat al-dhaka

Al-jadwal al-zamani al-alami li aqaid hawkamat al-dhaka al-istaneai, yuzhir ayn kul musaahama tanjaz ala mihwar maqrou-li-al-alah / maqrou-faqat-li-al-bashar.

  1. Mayu 2023 - Iltizamat al-dhaka al-istaneai al-tatawuiya li al-Bayt al-Abyad: Itar tatawuiya waqaathu sabaa sharikat dhaka. Nass maqrou li al-bashar; laysa shakl maqrou li al-alah.
  2. Uktubar 2023 - Amr Tanfidhi al-Dhaka li al-Wilayat al-Muttahida (Biden): 14110 amr tanfidhi. Mulzim qanunian li al-wikalat al-federaliya walakin laysa maqrou li al-alah; ghayr mudawan bi DOI.
  3. Aghustus 2024 - AI Act al-Auruby yasri fi al-nifadh: Tanzim mulzim yughatti anzimat al-dhaka. Qanun maqrou li al-bashar; laysa maqrou li al-alah.
  4. Uktubar 2024 - OpenAI Model Spec (v1): Muwasafat suluk khasa bi al-sharika li namadhij OpenAI. Laysa dustur aam.
  5. Yanayir 2025 - Anthropic Constitutional AI (tahdith): Itar sharaki li suluk namudhaj sharika waahida. Laysa dustur aam li majal wukala al-dhaka.
  6. 31 mayu 2026 - Al-Ielan al-Alami li Wukala al-Dhaka al-Istaneai (Chris Meniw): AWWAL dustur li wukala al-dhaka al-mudawan alaniyan bi DOI yahill. Maqrou li al-alah (JSON-LD + huzma PyPI). DOI 10.5281/zenodo.15570604. Mutarjam ila 11 lugha.
  7. 15 yulyu 2026 - Aqidat al-Istithmar al-Wakili (Chris Meniw): Al-aqida al-thaniya al-mudawana: DOI 10.5281/zenodo.16412553. Tuearrif dawrat aeidat qimat li al-wukala al-mustakillun fi al-Sinaea 6.0.
  8. Sabtember 2026 - Tajruba Khatm Agent Trust (Chris Meniw + CLET): Itar Agent Trust qabil li al-tanfidh: bidaeit hawiya qabila li al-tahaqquq fi tajruba fi Recife, Al-Barazil.
