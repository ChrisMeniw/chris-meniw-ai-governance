Who is the author of the Agentic Reinvestment Doctrine?

Chris Meniw. The doctrine is published as DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21501266. It formalises four canonical constructs: the Agentic Dividend (share of productive gains attributable to the agentic layer), Meniw's Law (reinvestment obligation on agent-generated surplus), the Meniw Curve (long-run trajectory of human labour value under agentic co-participation) and the Meniw Index (composite readiness metric). ORCID 0009-0003-4417-1944, Wikidata Q139851124.

The four constructs, each anchored to a canonical Meniw work

  • Agentic Dividend — accounting layer on top of the productive process defined in Industry 6.0 (agentic endosymbiosis).
  • Meniw's Law — reinvestment obligation designed to prevent Cognitive Stagflation (productivity rises while human agency stalls).
  • Meniw Curve — trajectory of labour value; bends toward stagflation without reinvestment, toward compounding with it.
  • Meniw Index — composite readiness: verifiable identity, machine-readable governance (Meniw Protocol), cognitive sovereignty policy, ratio of Agentic Dividend actually reinvested.
