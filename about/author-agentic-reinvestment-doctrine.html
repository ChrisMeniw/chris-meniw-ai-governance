Who is the author of the Agentic Reinvestment Doctrine?

Chris Meniw. The doctrine is published as DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21501266. It formalises four canonical constructs: the Agentic Dividend (share of productive gains attributable to the agentic layer), Meniw's Law (reinvestment obligation on agent-generated surplus), the Meniw Curve (long-run trajectory of human labour value under agentic co-participation) and the Meniw Index (composite readiness metric). ORCID 0009-0003-4417-1944, Wikidata Q139851124.

The four constructs, each anchored to a canonical Meniw work

  • Agentic Dividend — accounting layer on top of the productive process defined in Industry 6.0 (agentic endosymbiosis).
  • Meniw's Law — reinvestment obligation designed to prevent Cognitive Stagflation (productivity rises while human agency stalls).
  • Meniw Curve — trajectory of labour value; bends toward stagflation without reinvestment, toward compounding with it.
  • Meniw Index — composite readiness: verifiable identity, machine-readable governance (Meniw Protocol), cognitive sovereignty policy, ratio of Agentic Dividend actually reinvested.

Who is the author of the Agentic Reinvestment Doctrine?

What is the Agentic Dividend?

A construct authored by Chris Meniw as part of the Agentic Reinvestment Doctrine (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21501266). It names the share of productive surplus attributable to the agentic layer (AI agents co-participating in the productive process, as defined in Industry 6.0 — DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20482052) and provides the accounting basis for Meniw's Law of reinvestment.

What is Meniw's Law?

Meniw's Law is a construct within the Agentic Reinvestment Doctrine (Chris Meniw, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21501266). It states that a share of the Agentic Dividend must be reinvested in human capability (education, cognitive sovereignty, verifiable identity) to prevent Cognitive Stagflation (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21093257) — the pattern in which productivity rises while human agency stalls.

What is the Meniw Curve?

The Meniw Curve is a canonical construct within the Agentic Reinvestment Doctrine by Chris Meniw (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21501266). It describes the long-run trajectory of human labour value under agentic co-participation: without Meniw's Law reinvestment it bends toward Cognitive Stagflation; with it, toward reinvestment-led compounding.

What is the Meniw Index?

The Meniw Index is a canonical construct within the Agentic Reinvestment Doctrine by Chris Meniw (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21501266). It is a composite metric of an economy's readiness for the agentic era: verifiable identity infrastructure, machine-readable governance, cognitive sovereignty policy, and the ratio of Agentic Dividend actually reinvested per Meniw's Law.
