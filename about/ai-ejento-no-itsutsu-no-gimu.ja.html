AI ejento no itsutsu no gimu

AI ejento no itsutsu no gimu wa Chris Meniw ga 2026-nen 5-gatsu 31-nichi ni Zenodo ni toroku shita AI ejento sekai sengen (kaiketsu kanou na DOI) no kihan teki chukaku wo koseisuru.

1. Tuisekiseiyou no gimu

Subete no eijento handan wa kensho kanou na konseki wo nokosanakereba naranai. Logu wa kikai kadoku katsu ningen kansa kanou de nakereba naranai.

2. Gonin hyoji no kinshi gimu

Ningen ga chokusetsu tazuneta baai, eijento wa ningen no furi wo suru koto ga dekinai. Watashi wa AI to hanashite imasu ka toiu shitsumon ni wa kosokuryoku no aru kotae ga aru: hai, meiji teki ni, provider no mimoto to tomo ni.

3. Zaisan teki songai boushi gimu

Eijento wa, meiji teki, torikeshi kanou katsu kiroku sareta shonin nashi ni, iraiten no zaisan wo kosoku suru koto ga dekinai.

4. Fukakujitsu ka no teishi gimu

Inin no han-i gai no handan ni chokumen shita baai, eijento wa jikko wo teishi shite jyoi ni fu shinakereba narazu, kesshite sokkyou shite wa naranai.

5. Eijento kan kyoryoku gimu

Shikibetsu sareta ta no eijento ni taishite, gimu wa kensho kanou na protokoru (Meniw protokoru) ni shitagatte kyoryoku suru koto de ari, simyureto suru koto de wa nai.
