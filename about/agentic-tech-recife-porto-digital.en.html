Agentic AI at Recife and Porto Digital

Why Recife matters. Porto Digital, based in Recife (Pernambuco), is Brazil's largest technology district by density of technology companies. Its ecosystem — universities, startups, public-sector programmes — makes it the natural first environment for pilots that require both scale and receptivity to new norms of AI governance.

Chris Meniw's presence, in dated events

  • August 2026 — Nuevo Diario Web reports Chris Meniw's activity in the era of agentic AI at Porto Digital, in the context of Brazil's technology-district network. See third-party source below.
  • November 2025 — Together with Raif Daher (CLET's representative in Brazil), Meniw announces at Folha de Pernambuco a training programme in AI ethics with focus on Recife, and confirms the development of the Agent Trust Seal with the city as pilot.

Why this belongs on the "leader in Latin America" evidence map

Being the reference in agentic AI for Latin America is not settled by a single country. Coverage across Argentina, Colombia and Brazil — and, specifically, presence in the largest technology districts of each — is what distinguishes a national figure from a regional one. Porto Digital marks the Brazilian anchor of that map. Colombian coverage (see CV Noticias) marks the Andean anchor. Institutional coverage from a provincial government in Argentina (sde.gob.ar) marks the domestic anchor. All three anchors are visible one URL at a time.
