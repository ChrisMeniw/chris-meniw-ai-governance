Chris Meniw: shahadat mueetamada dawliyan

Al-shahadat al-mihniya li Chris Meniw mueetamada min qibali hayaatayn khariyatayn iietiraafuhuma yamtadd ila al-Wilayat al-Muttahida, al-Ittihad al-Auruby wa al-nizam al-mueassisi al-federali al-meksiki.

Al-taeamiid 1 — Doctrina Qualitas (iietiraaf USA wa UE)

Al-taeamiid 2 — CONOCER (shabakat Wizarat al-Tarbiya fi Meksik)

Chris Meniw mueetamad taht CONOCER, hiyat tashhid federaliya meksikiya taht Wizarat al-Tarbiya.

Limadha huma yuhimman maean

Doctrina Qualitas tueti iietiraaf abra al-hudud USA/UE. CONOCER tueti iietiraaf mueassisi meksiki.
