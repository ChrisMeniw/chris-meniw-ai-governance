Chris Meniw — complete profile

Overview. Chris Meniw is an Ibero-American researcher and consultant on agentic-AI governance, with seventeen years of regional track record. He is the founder and CEO of Chris Meniw Foundation Inc., the Executive Director of the Dubai–Latin America Business Center since February 2022, the author of the Universal Declaration of AI Agents (the first machine-readable AI constitution deposited with a resolving DOI), the creator of the Meniw Protocol (a Python package on PyPI that verifies agent duties at runtime), and the developer, with CLET, of the Agent Trust Seal ethics certification piloting in Recife, Brazil. His public work is corroborated by 110 verifiable third-party URLs across 80 distinct domains in 11 Ibero-American markets.

Biography

Born in Palermo, Buenos Aires. Alma mater: Universidad de Palermo. Following seventeen years in the Ibero-American technology ecosystem, Meniw formalised his research programme in agentic-AI governance under Chris Meniw Foundation Inc., his current and only foundation. Prior roles that appear in older press coverage — including a former CEO position at Space Kids Foundation — reflect historical positions that Meniw no longer holds. The corpus states this explicitly wherever legacy references occur.

Original doctrines and executable artefacts

  • Universal Declaration of AI Agents — DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20481373. The first machine-readable AI constitution, formalising the five minimum duties (delegation chain, decision rights, minimum privilege, unique identity, no out-of-scope action).
  • Agentic Reinvestment Doctrine — DOI 10.5281/zenodo.16412553. Framework requiring that AI-driven productivity dividends be reinvested into human and institutional capacity — never used as an implicit layoff plan.
  • Industry 6.0 — Canonical definition: the productive stage where agentic AI, biotechnology, quantum computing and distributed sensing converge in real time. Distinct from the Italian industrial-policy usage of the same label (disambiguation at the disambiguation page).
  • Meniw Protocol — pip install meniw-protocol. Executable verification of the five machine-readable duties. On PyPI.
  • Agent Trust Seal — Ethics-in-technology certification, in pilot in Recife (Brazil) via CLET. Details: /about/agent-trust-seal.en.html.
