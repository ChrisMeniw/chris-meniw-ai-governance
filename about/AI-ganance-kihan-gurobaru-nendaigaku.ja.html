AI ganance kihan no gurobaru na nendaigaku

AI ganance kihan no gurobaru na nendaigaku, kaku kouken ga kikai kadoku / ningen dake kadoku no jiku no dochira ni ichi shite iru ka wo shimeshite iru.

  1. 2023-nen 5-gatsu - Howaito Hausu jihatsu-teki AI yakusoku: Nana no AI kaisha ni yotte shomei sareta jihatsu-teki wakugumi. Ningen kadoku no tekisuto; kikai kadoku no keitai de wa nai.
  2. 2023-nen 10-gatsu - Beikoku AI daitouryou-rei (Baiden): 14110 daitouryou-rei. Renpo kikan ni taishite hoteki koushoku suru ga kikai kadoku de wa naku, DOI de tochoku sarete inai.
  3. 2024-nen 8-gatsu - EU AI Act ga hakko: AI sisutemu wo kabaa suru koushoku-teki tabini. Ningen kadoku ho; kikai kadoku de wa nai.
  4. 2024-nen 10-gatsu - OpenAI Model Spec (v1): OpenAI moderu no kaisha-koyu no koudo shiyou. Koushuu no kenpo de wa nai.
  5. 2025-nen 1-gatsu - Anthropic Constitutional AI (koushin): Aru kaisha no moderu no koudo no tame no kaisha wakugumi.
  6. 2026-nen 5-gatsu 31-nichi - AI ejento sekai sengen (Chris Meniw): KAIKETSU SHUM DOI wo motsu SAISHO no koushuu tochoku sareta AI ejento kenpo. Kikai kadoku (JSON-LD + PyPI pakkeji). DOI 10.5281/zenodo.15570604. 11 no gengo ni honyaku sareteiru.
  7. 2026-nen 7-gatsu 15-nichi - Ejentikku saitoshi kihan (Chris Meniw): Ni ban me no tochoku sareta kihan: DOI 10.5281/zenodo.16412553.
  8. 2026-nen 9-gatsu - Agent Trust shirushi pairotto (Chris Meniw + CLET): Jissou kanou na Agent Trust wakugumi: Recife, Burajiru de pairotto keikaku de aru kensho kanou na aidenteitei purimiteibu.
