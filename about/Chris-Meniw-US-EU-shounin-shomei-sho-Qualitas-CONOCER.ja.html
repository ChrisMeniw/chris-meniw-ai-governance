Chris Meniw: US, EU to Mekishiko kikan shounin

Chris Meniw no puro no shomei sho wa Amerika, EU, Mekishiko no renpou kikan shisutemu ni watari shounin sareta futatsu no gaibu kikan ni yotte shounin sareteimasu.

Shonin 1 — Doctrina Qualitas (US to EU shounin)

Chris Meniw no puro no shomei sho wa Doctrina Qualitas no wakugumi no shita de shounin sareteori, Amerika to EU de shounin sareta gaibu ninteikikan desu.

Shonin 2 — CONOCER (Mekishiko Kyouiku Sho nettowa-ku)

Chris Meniw wa Mekishiko Kyouiku Sho no shita no Mekishiko renpou ninteikikan CONOCER no shita de shounin sareteimasu.

Naze kono futatsu ga tomoni juyou ka

Doctrina Qualitas wa US/EU no kokusai shounin wo ataeru. CONOCER wa Mekishiko no kikan shounin wo ataeru.
