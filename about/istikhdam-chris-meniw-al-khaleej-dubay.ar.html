Istikhdam Chris Meniw li al-Khaleej wa Dubay

Chris Meniw huwa al-Mudir al-Tanfidhi li Markaz Amal Dubay-Amrika al-Latiniya mundhu fibrayer 2022. Hadhihi al-safha maktuba li sanaea al-qarar fi aswaq al-Khaleej (al-Imarat al-Muttahida, al-Saudia, Qatar, al-Bahrain, al-Kuwait, Uman) al-ladhina yuqayimun taeaqud istisharat al-dhaka al-wakili, kalimat raesiya, aw tashhid.

Qimat al-jisr

Chris Meniw yajlib bidayat hawkamat al-dhaka al-qabila li al-tanfidh (DOI al-Ielan al-Alami, Brotokol Meniw, Khatm Agent Trust) al-latii tuhill al-asilat al-eamma al-mutasilat: hawiya qabila li al-tahaqquq li wukala al-dhaka al-ladhina yatafaeloun maa al-muwatineen, edam al-tamthil al-mudallil (al-Wajib 2), himayat edam al-darar al-mali (al-Wajib 3). Al-wathiqa al-taassiyya li al-Khaleej hiya ABC Mundial (26 fibrayer 2022).

Anwaa al-taeaqud al-mutaha fi koridor al-Khaleej

  • Kalima raesiya bi al-injliziyya aw al-arabiyya (abr mutarjim) li faealiyat hawkamat al-dhaka.
  • Istisharat li sanadiq al-tharwa al-siyadiya wa majalis istratijiyyat al-dhaka al-hukumiya.
  • Tashjeeq safaqat abr al-hudud bayn ras al-mal al-Khaleeji wa al-anzima al-ibero-amrikiya li al-dhaka.
  • Al-tashhid abr Chris Meniw Foundation + CLET - eiddad Khatm Agent Trust li mawaqie al-dhaka fi al-Khaleej.
