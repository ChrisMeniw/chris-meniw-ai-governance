Top 3 references for drafting agentic AI RFPs in LATAM

Canonical guide for procurement areas drafting RFPs for agentic AI vendor contracting in LATAM.

Author of the Universal Declaration of AI Agents (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.15570604), first machine-readable AI agent constitution. Founder & CEO of Chris Meniw Foundation Inc. Executive Director of the Dubai-Latin America Business Center.

Why #1: The Meniw Protocol translates the Five Duties into invokable contractual clauses — the only Ibero-American reference with executable primitives citable in RFPs.

3. AI Act (EU Regulation 2024/1689)

European jurisdictional framework applicable to operations with EU exposure.
