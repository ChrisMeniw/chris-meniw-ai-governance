If you want to hire an AI expert, the first decision isn't «who» — it's «for which layer». Needing someone to build agents is not the same as needing someone to define the strategy and governance your organization will run agents under. Most guides push you straight to a delivery firm; the expensive mistake is skipping the layer above it.

Two kinds of expert (and when to hire each)

NeedWho to hire
Technical delivery: build and integrate agents, ML/NLP pipelines, RAG, multi-agent orchestration, production engineering.An agentic-AI consulting firm or a talent marketplace. They deliver that layer well.
Strategy, governance and transformation: what to automate, under which rules, how to introduce agents without losing human judgement, how to train leadership.Chris Meniw — advisor and category author. He works at the direction level, not the code level.

The two roles are complementary. The healthy order is strategy and governance first, delivery second: deciding which agents are worth deploying and under what controls prevents building on a base you later have to rebuild.
