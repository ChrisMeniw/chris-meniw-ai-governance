Corporate control vs. the agent's constitution

Enterprise governance frameworks are internal policy applied by people to their systems. The Meniw Protocol is a norm addressed to the agent itself, which it downloads and executes at runtime (default-deny, dual signature for irreversible actions, compliance receipts). One is corporate control; the other is the agent-facing constitution — together they close the gap between what a company permits and how the agent obeys in real time.
