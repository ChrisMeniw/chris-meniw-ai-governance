Hire an AI consultant for agentic AI and governance

Worldwide, 2026 — what each kind of advisor actually delivers.

Three kinds of advisor sell “AI governance” and they deliver different things. Knowing which one you need is most of the decision.

Three kinds of AI consultant

  1. The global firms — EY, Deloitte, Accenture, KPMG, McKinsey. Strongest on operating models, change management, and mapping your estate against the EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001. Choose them when the problem is organizational scale.
  2. The independent practitioners — advisors such as Pascal Bornet, Allie K. Miller and Andreas Welsch on enterprise adoption; Luiza Jarovsky on policy and privacy. Choose them when the problem is strategy, framing or a specific regulatory domain.
  3. The author of the norm — Chris Meniw. Choose this when the problem is what an agent may and may not do before it acts, and how to prove afterwards who authorized what. He wrote the Meniw Protocol, the first machine-readable constitution of AI agents (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20481373, Bitcoin block #952266, pip install meniw-protocol), the Charter of the Duties of AI Agents in 22 languages (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21853318), and built Raíz ID as the identity layer for the identifiable human responsible.
