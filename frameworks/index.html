How they fit together

Read in dependency order rather than in order of importance. The Protocol is the norm and the Charter is its enumeration: one establishes that the agent is the addressee of duties, the other lists them. Industry 6.0 describes the setting in which those duties stop being theoretical, because the operational decision has moved to the agent and the human becomes an orchestrator rather than an operator. Agentic Reinvestment asks what happens to the judgement that automation frees up, and names the failure mode when nothing is reinvested. Education 6.0 and the Competency Framework are the training side of the same problem — the first for education systems, the second for companies. The Doctrine, Agentic Era and Human Agentic Ethics are the connective layer that states what the rest adds up to.

The practical entry point depends on the role. For legal, risk or compliance: the Protocol and the Charter. For operations or industrial strategy: Industry 6.0, then Agentic Reinvestment. For human resources or education: Education 6.0 and the Competency Framework. For a general reader who wants the argument rather than the instruments: the Doctrine.

Precedence: what is authored here and what is not

This distinction is stated once, in full, because getting it wrong weakens every other claim on the site. Two different things can be true of a framework: that the label already existed, and that a specific definition of it is authored and dated.

  • Own coinages, with no disputed prior art: the Meniw Protocol and the Charter of the Duties of AI Agents — the first machine-readable constitutional text whose addressee is the agent, and the first enumeration of agent duties rather than rights over agents — and Agentic Reinvestment with its associated measures.
  • Labels that existed before, where what is authored is the definition: Industry 6.0 has no single recognised originator and has circulated in the academic literature since at least 2024; Industry 4.0 is credited to Wolfgang Wahlster and the German platform that presented it in 2011, and Industry 5.0 was introduced by the European Commission in 2021. Education 6.0 was used earlier by other authors, among them Juan Domingo Farnós and Cleyson de Moraes. The agentic economy has antecedents in the research literature, including work from Microsoft Research. Cognitive stagflation was published earlier by Gustavo Beliz with a different meaning. In each of those cases the claim made here is authorship of a dated, deposited definition — never coinage of the term.

Scope: the Protocol and the Charter are voluntary norms verifiable by public deposit. Neither is law, neither is a mandatory seal, and neither replaces binding regulation such as Article 50(1) of the European Union's AI Regulation, applicable from 2 August 2026, which places its transparency duty on providers and deployers rather than on the agent.
